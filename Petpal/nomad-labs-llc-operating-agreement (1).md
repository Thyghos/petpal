# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF NOMAD LABS LLC

**A Florida Single-Member Limited Liability Company**

---

**THIS OPERATING AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is adopted and made effective as of _______________, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by **Emilio John Alecci**, an individual (the "Member"), as the sole member of **Nomad Labs LLC**, a Florida limited liability company (the "Company").

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Member has formed the Company by filing Articles of Organization with the Florida Department of State on _______________, 2026, Document Number _______________; and

**WHEREAS**, the Member desires to set forth the rights, duties, and management of the Company in this Agreement to document the Company's operations, reinforce the limited liability protection afforded under Florida law, and clearly establish ownership of Company assets including intellectual property;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Member adopts this Agreement as follows:

---

## ARTICLE I — FORMATION AND NAME

**1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed as a Florida limited liability company under the Florida Revised Limited Liability Company Act (Chapter 605, Florida Statutes).

**1.2 Name.** The name of the Company is **Nomad Labs LLC**.

**1.3 Principal Office.** The Company's principal office is located at 5353 Gulf Blvd APT A304, St. Pete Beach, Florida 33706, or such other address as the Member may designate from time to time.

**1.4 Registered Agent.** The registered agent and registered office are stated in the Articles of Organization, as may be amended.

**1.5 Term.** The Company shall continue perpetually unless dissolved by the Member or by operation of law.

---

## ARTICLE II — PURPOSE AND PRODUCTS

**2.1 Business Purpose.** The Company is organized to engage in any lawful activity for which limited liability companies may be formed under Florida law. The Company's principal business activity is the development, marketing, sale, and operation of software-as-a-service and consumer software products.

**2.2 Current Products.** As of the Effective Date, the Company owns and operates the following products:

(a) **Revflo** — an SMS retention and automation platform for wineries, tasting rooms, and vineyards, accessible at revflo.app.

(b) **PetPal** — a pet-focused consumer application distributed through the Apple App Store.

**2.3 Future Products.** The Member may, at the Member's sole discretion, launch, acquire, or dispose of additional products under the Company without amendment to this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE III — MEMBERSHIP AND CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**3.1 Sole Member.** Emilio John Alecci is the sole member of the Company and owns **100%** of the Ownership Interest in the Company.

**3.2 Initial Capital Contribution.** The Member's initial capital contribution consists of:

(a) The existing source code, intellectual property, domain names, and associated assets for the Revflo software platform;

(b) The existing source code, intellectual property, Apple App Store presence, and associated assets for the PetPal application;

(c) Services rendered and to be rendered by the Member in developing and operating the Company's products.

The value of these contributions is the fair market value of the contributed assets and services.

**3.3 Additional Capital Contributions.** The Member may, at the Member's discretion, make additional capital contributions to the Company. No such contributions are required.

**3.4 No Other Members.** The Company has no other members as of the Effective Date. Should the Member elect to admit additional members in the future, this Agreement shall be amended or replaced accordingly, and the Member shall obtain a new EIN reflecting multi-member tax classification.

---

## ARTICLE IV — MANAGEMENT

**4.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is managed by the Member as the sole Managing Member.

**4.2 Authority.** The Member has full authority to act on behalf of the Company, including but not limited to:

(a) Entering into contracts;

(b) Opening and managing bank accounts;

(c) Hiring and compensating employees and independent contractors;

(d) Acquiring and disposing of Company assets;

(e) Borrowing money on behalf of the Company;

(f) Making distributions to the Member;

(g) Amending this Agreement;

(h) Dissolving the Company;

(i) Launching, acquiring, or disposing of products operated by the Company;

(j) Executing any documents necessary or advisable for the Company's business.

**4.3 Delegation.** The Member may delegate specific authorities to employees, contractors, or agents of the Company. Delegation does not transfer ownership.

---

## ARTICLE V — DISTRIBUTIONS AND TAXATION

**5.1 Tax Classification.** The Company is a single-member LLC treated as a **disregarded entity** for federal income tax purposes. The Company's income, deductions, and credits flow through to the Member's personal tax return on Schedule C (or Schedule E, depending on activity type).

**5.2 No Corporate Election.** The Member has not elected, and does not currently intend to elect, to have the Company treated as a corporation (including S-corporation) for tax purposes. Any such election in the future shall be documented in an amendment to this Agreement.

**5.3 Distributions.** The Member may take distributions from the Company at any time, in any amount, subject to maintaining sufficient Company reserves to meet operating expenses and contingent liabilities.

**5.4 Tax Payments.** The Member is responsible for paying all federal, state, and self-employment taxes arising from the Company's pass-through income.

**5.5 Bank Accounts.** All Company funds shall be maintained in accounts in the Company's name. The Member shall not commingle personal funds with Company funds. All Company business shall be conducted through Company bank accounts.

---

## ARTICLE VI — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**6.1 Company Ownership of Work Product.** All intellectual property created, developed, or acquired for or on behalf of the Company's business — including software source code, designs, inventions, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, customer lists, business methods, documentation, marketing materials, written content, social media content created for Company products, domain names, mobile applications, and any derivative works thereof (collectively, "Company IP") — is the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

**6.2 Member Assignment.** The Member hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to any Company IP that the Member has created, is creating, or will create in connection with the Company's business, in perpetuity throughout the world. This assignment specifically includes but is not limited to:

(a) The Revflo software platform, source code, user interface, and all derivative works;

(b) The PetPal mobile application, source code, designs, and all derivative works;

(c) The "Revflo," "PetPal," and "Nomad Labs" names, marks, and associated trademarks;

(d) The "revflo.app" domain and any related subdomains;

(e) Any domains associated with the PetPal application;

(f) The @revfloapp Instagram account and any other social media accounts operated on behalf of Company products;

(g) The PetPal Apple App Store presence, including app metadata, screenshots, descriptions, and any associated developer account assets specifically pertaining to PetPal;

(h) Customer contracts, customer lists, and business relationships acquired through the Company's products;

(i) Marketing materials, written content, documentation, and business methods created for or used by the Company.

**6.3 Employee and Contractor Work Product.** Any intellectual property created by employees, contractors, or other non-member contributors in the course of work performed for the Company is also the sole property of the Company. As a condition of employment or engagement, non-member contributors shall execute work-made-for-hire and IP assignment agreements confirming this ownership.

**6.4 Further Assurances.** The Member and any non-member contributors shall execute any documents reasonably necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of Company IP.

**6.5 Works Made for Hire.** To the extent any Company IP constitutes "works made for hire" under the U.S. Copyright Act, such works are hereby deemed works made for hire, and the Company is deemed the author and owner thereof.

---

## ARTICLE VII — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Limited Liability.** The Member's liability is limited to the Member's investment in the Company. The Member is not personally liable for the Company's debts, obligations, or liabilities solely by reason of being a member, provided the Member maintains the formalities required by this Agreement and Florida law, including:

(a) Maintaining separate Company bank accounts;

(b) Not commingling Company funds with personal funds;

(c) Respecting the Company as a separate legal entity;

(d) Executing contracts in the Company's name rather than personally.

**7.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify the Member and any employees or agents against any liability, loss, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from actions taken on behalf of the Company, except to the extent arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of fiduciary duty.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — DISSOLUTION AND SUCCESSION

**8.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon:

(a) The Member's written election to dissolve the Company;

(b) The Member's death, unless the Member's estate plan provides for the continuation of the Company (see Section 8.3);

(c) A court order dissolving the Company;

(d) Any other event causing dissolution under Florida law.

**8.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Company shall pay its debts and distribute any remaining assets to the Member or the Member's estate.

**8.3 Succession upon Member's Death.** The Member shall designate the beneficiary of the Member's Ownership Interest in the Company through the Member's will, revocable living trust, or other estate planning instrument. The Member acknowledges that this Agreement alone cannot effectuate transfer on death and that a properly executed will or trust is required for the Ownership Interest to pass as intended. The Member shall execute such estate planning documents within a reasonable time following the Effective Date.

**8.4 Incapacity.** In the event of the Member's permanent incapacity, the Member's designated attorney-in-fact (under a durable power of attorney) shall have authority to manage the Company on the Member's behalf. The Member shall execute a durable power of attorney as part of the Member's estate planning.

---

## ARTICLE IX — MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement regarding the Company.

**9.2 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Member.

**9.3 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**9.4 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

**9.5 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Electronic signatures have the same force and effect as original signatures.
